# 12 · Progression

There are **three layers** of progression, and none of them is levels:
1. **The character** (AW improvement)
2. **The holdings** (the playbook's signature asset)
3. **The world** (Lean, Rep, the World Clock)

## 1. Character: experience and improvement (AW)
- **Experience** is marked when you:
  - roll a **highlighted** stat
  - **reset Hx** (+4 to +1, or at session end, −3 to 0)
  - use moves that say "*mark experience*" (*Eager to know* when you fail the advised course; *Inspiring* for helpers; insight)
- **5 experience gives one improvement** from your playbook's list (data‑imported from AW). Typical options:
  - +1 to a stat (each playbook caps it)
  - a new playbook move
  - a move from another playbook
  - a signature‑asset upgrade (a new vehicle; a garage and crew; a gang option; holding options; a workspace)
  - a **holding and wealth** (Chopper, Driver)
- **After 5 improvements, the advanced list opens**:
  - +1 to any stat (max +3)
  - **retire your character to safety**: a campaign ending you choose, with an epilogue
  - **create a second character**: switch to a crew member, keeping the world
  - **change playbook**: you keep your history and the world, and get the new playbook's sheet
  - **advance 3 basic moves**, then the **other 3**: unlocks **12+** results (AW's advanced basic moves)
- **The pace**: about one improvement every **2–3 months** of play, so about 15–20 over a full campaign.

## 2. Holdings: what the playbook owns
Each playbook's signature asset upgrades through **improvements**, **barter** and **projects** (workspace time):

| Playbook | Asset | Upgrade path |
|---|---|---|
| Driver | Car and convoy | Frame swaps, +speed/handling/armor/massive, strengths, ornaments, **2 more cars** (*Collector*), a **battle vehicle** (*My other car is a tank*) |
| Chopper | Gang | AW gang options (well‑armed, well‑armored, disciplined, nomadic, self‑sufficient), size steps through recruitment, a **holding** |
| Hardholder | Hold | AW holding options (size, gang, armory, walls, manufactory, market…), and the **caravan network** |
| Hocus | Followers | Size, fortunes options, and **sites of worship** in other holds |
| Savvyhead | Workspace | Workspace types (garage, lab, infirmary, darkroom, growing space, machinist's floor), **projects** |
| Quarantine | Stasis | Stasis options (medlab, armory, sealed vault…), and **memories** unlocked |

## 3. World progression
- **Rep** (in each hold, −3 to +5): how people *talk about you*. It gates recruiting, gigs, prices (±5% a step), and the Seat. It rises with gigs, relieved wants, Sights and defending the hold. It falls with raids, broken deals and *loud* violence.
- **Lean**: territory ([07](07-economy.md#investment-and-lean-uwnhs-allegiance-aws-way)).
- **Map knowledge**: charts and Sights. A completion journal that the News buys from.
- **Disciplines**: learned at maelstrom sites ([04](04-moves-engine.md#weird-disciplines-inindos-magic-schools-translated)).
- **The World Clock**: progress against Last (or toward Last) is the campaign meter.

## Gear
- **Weapons and armor** carry AW tags ([09](09-combat.md#harm-reference-from-aw), [13](13-content-tables.md)). They don't scale: a 3‑harm shotgun in month 1 is a 3‑harm shotgun in month 60. Progression comes from *options* (ap, area, hi‑tech), not inflating numbers.
- **Armor** is 0 to 2 for people, as in AW. "*2‑armor is serious body armor.*"
- **Oddments and luxe gear** are social progression: valuable, worn, and a +1 to Hot in the right company.
