# 11 · Crew and Bonds

> UWNH gave you mates with wages. Inindo gave you companions recruited at inns. AW gives you **Hx**, and NPCs who are *human*: they have a name, a look and a want.

## Recruitment
- **Where**: hold **bars** (UWNH's cafe, Inindo's inn), straggler camps, defeated rivals ("*I surrender!*"), rescued prisoners, maelstrom sites (weird masters' students), and **sub‑faction chapters**. At Standing +2, each sub‑faction offers recruits of particular playbook archetypes: the Teeth have Choppers and Gunluggers, the Keepers have Angels and Hocuses, the Wheelwrights have Savvyheads ([18](18-sub-factions.md)). A sub‑faction recruit carries that sub‑faction's Standing with them, which works both ways.
- **Who**: named NPCs, drawn from the roster of **playbook archetypes** ([06](06-playbooks.md#the-rest-of-the-roster-crew-archetypes-and-unlockable-protagonists)) plus non‑playbook folk: drivers, gunners, cooks, hands.
- **Gate**: Inindo gated recruiting on *experience* ("*The more experience you have, the more likely someone will join you*"). We gate it on **Rep** in that hold and on **Hx**:
  - **Rep ≥ their bar**: they'll talk business.
  - **A recruiting scene** is a *seduce or manipulate* (give them a reason: barter, a cause, their want) or a *go aggro* (for gangs). A 7–9 needs an assurance, which becomes a small quest.
- **Hx questions on joining**: each recruit asks *you* one question from their playbook ("*Which one of you do I figure is doomed to self‑destruction?*" as the Angel asks). Your answer sets their Hx with you and becomes a Director **fact**.

## Crew size
- **Named crew**: up to **6**, who appear in scenes, skirmishes and the Hx box. The 3 in your **party** come into skirmishes.
- **Hands**: an abstract headcount that fills rig crew minimums and makes up your **gang** (*small* / *medium*…). Hands have no names until something happens to them. Then the Director names them (AW: "*name everyone*").

## Loyalty: Hx, wants and pack alpha
- **Hx** (−2 to +3) is your bond with each named crew member. It moves with harm, healing, session end, shared danger and paying them well.
- **Every NPC has a want** (one AW resource or relationship). Feed it and Hx rises. Ignore it for 3 or more months and they make a **want move**: they ask, then demand, then leave or act on their own.
- **Pay**: either a crew cut (0.5 barter a month each) or **shares** (10% of cargo profits, split). Late pay costs −1 Hx each month.
- **Pack alpha bids** (from the Chopper, generalised to all crews at the Director's discretion). When crew Hx is low and chow, pay or success fails, a crew member makes a bid, *idle or serious*, to take over. You answer it with *go aggro*, *seduce or manipulate*, single combat, or by giving in. UWNH's mutiny, played as a move.

## Rivals
- **Training rivals** (Inindo): wanderers on their own Train or Seek work arcs who challenge you to **single combat** at maelstrom sites and bars. Win, and they surrender, then join, owe you, or leave with Hx +1. Lose, and they take something.
- **Mirror protagonists**: the other five launch playbooks, as named NPCs (see [06](06-playbooks.md#common-to-every-protagonist)).

## Intimacy (off‑screen, mechanical)
- **Trigger**: with an NPC at Hx +2 or more, a **[Stay the night]** option can appear in private scenes, once the NPC's want, mood and the fiction allow. NPCs can also *offer*.
- **Presentation**: a **cut to black**, then a short line of morning‑after prose in the playbook voice. Nothing explicit, ever. With the **Intimacy** setting off, even that line is skipped. The mechanics still apply, and the cut shows only as an Hx change.
- **Mechanics**: the **playbook sex moves and specials** apply as written in AW. For example:
  - Chopper: they write Hx+3 with you.
  - Hocus: you each hold 1 for help/interfere at a distance.
  - Quarantine: roll+sharp to ask them questions.
  - Driver: roll+cool to see if it's "*no big deal*".
  - Hardholder: give gifts worth 1‑barter at no cost.
- **Hard rules**:
  - Adults only.
  - Never as a result of brainer control, *go aggro*, captivity or debt.
  - An NPC under a Director *capture* or Tum Tum's ownership can never be an intimacy option.
  - A Hocus's *Frenzy* option and the Brainer's transfixion moves are **non‑sexual** in this game.
- **Consequences**: jealousy and rivalry within the crew (Hx shifts), threat hooks (someone wants them back), and real loss if they die. AW never makes intimacy free.

## Crew death and memory
- Named crew who die are remembered: their **grave** is placed on the map, and their Hx becomes a **grief** modifier (−1 ongoing to Hot for a month, or +1 Hard).
- If your PC dies, a crew member with Hx +2 or more can carry on (AW's "*create a second character*"). They inherit the rigs and the gang (with a *pack alpha* bid), and their Hx with the dead PC becomes their motivation in the arc.
