# 09 · Combat

There are three combat modes, all resolved with **AW battle moves** and **harm as established**. None of them uses hit points.

| Mode | Lineage | Scale | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Skirmish** | Inindo's grid battles, UWNH's duel | 1–4 PCs/crew against NPCs and small gangs | Hold fights, salvage dives, boarding actions, ambushes |
| **Road war** | UWNH's fleet battles | Up to 6 rigs a side | Raids, chases, escorts |
| **Gang war** | Inindo's province wars, AW gang rules | Gangs and holds | Sieges, defending your hold, warlord conquest |

## Harm reference (from AW)
**Weapon tags**: *hand* (adjacent), *close* (up to 4 tiles), *far* (up to 10 tiles), *ap* (ignores armor), *area*, *loud* (draws attention: a Director move), *messy*, *reload* (needs an action after firing), *refill*, *autofire*, *infinite*, *valuable*, *hi‑tech*, *s‑harm* (stun, no wounds), *slow*, *numerous*, *remote*.

**Harm as established** = the weapon's harm − the target's armor (± gang size steps).

**PCs (and key crew)** have the harm clock:

| Segment | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 3:00, 6:00 | Hurt. Heals by itself (one segment per month, or faster at an Infirmary) |
| 9:00 | Wounded. **Gets worse each month unless stabilized** |
| 10:00, 11:00 | Badly hurt. Gets worse without help |
| 12:00 | Life untenable: come back with −1hard / come back with +1weird / change playbook / die |

**NPCs** use AW's tier table:
- 1: cosmetic, pain, fear if they're likely to be afraid of pain
- 2: wounds, unconscious, bad pain, broken bones, shock; likely fatal, occasionally immediately
- 3: 50/50 immediately fatal
- 4: usually fatal at once
- 5+: fatal and destructive

The Director *rolls* for 50/50s, as AW says to "*disclaim decision‑making*". The result is shown on screen as a coin.

**Gangs**:
- 1: a few injuries
- 2: many injuries, a couple of fatalities
- 3: widespread injuries, several fatalities
- 4: widespread serious injuries, many fatalities
- 5+: few survivors

Cohesion depends on the leader:
- A strong leader who is present: holds through **4‑harm**.
- A weak or absent leader: holds through **3**.
- Weak *and* absent: holds through **1–2**.
- No leader: holds through **1**.

**Vehicles**:
- 1: cosmetic
- 2: functional
- 3: serious
- 4: breakdown
- 5: destroyed

Harm "blows through" to passengers at −1 per tier (2 functional = 1‑harm through), as the Director judges.

## Mode 1: Skirmish (grid)
**Board**: about 16×10 tiles, with cover (−1 harm from *far*), high ground (+1 to *lay down fire*), hazards (Terrain threats) and objectives.

**Deployment**: Inindo's **Lineup** becomes three stances. You place your PC and up to 3 crew:
- **Front**: engages in *hand*/*close* range first.
- **Flank**: can move twice on the first round.
- **Overwatch**: starts in *stand overwatch* for its best ally.

**Before the fight (optional)**: **read a sitch** for questions that give +1 forward; and the **subterfuge** moves if the fight starts as a hunt:
- *bait a trap*
- *hunt prey*
- *escape a hunter*
- *turn the tables*

These decide *where* the fight happens and who starts with the advantage.

**Rounds**:
1. **Player phase.** Each PC and crew member moves (up to 4 tiles, 2 in dunes or rubble) and makes **one battle move**:

| Battle move | On the grid |
|---|---|
| **Single combat** (+hard) | Engage one adjacent enemy one‑on‑one. Exchange harm. 10+: both of *inflict terrible harm (+1)* and *suffer little harm (−1)*. 7–9: one of them. Then choose whether to **fight on or end it** (the opponent chooses too). This is also UWNH's **duel**: a leader's single combat can end a whole boarding action. |
| **Chaotic free‑for‑all** (+cool) | You're in a scrum of 3 or more engaged units. 10+: choose 3. 7–9: choose 2. Options: avoid harm / protect someone / +1 harm to the chaos / −1 harm to the chaos / one free short action. |
| **Assault a secure position** (+hard) | Attack an enemy in cover or a fortification. Choose: +1 harm / −1 harm / force your way in / force them to withdraw. |
| **Seize something by force** (+hard) | Take an objective tile or item. Choose: +1 harm / −1 harm / definite control / it's undamaged. |
| **Defend something you hold** (+hard) | Hold a tile, person or item. Choose: +1 harm / −1 harm / hold decisively / impress, dismay or frighten them (a morale check). |
| **Lay down fire** (+hard) | Ranged suppression in a cone. 10+: choose 3. 7–9: choose 2. Miss: choose 1. Options: covering fire (an ally moves freely) / supporting fire (an ally gets +1 choice) / suppressing fire (a target can't move or act freely) / an opportune shot (harm −1 to a target in reach). Uses ammo. |
| **Stand overwatch** (+cool) | A reaction: whoever attacks your ally gets shot. 10+: shoot *first*, or for +1 harm. Miss: warn your ally only. |
| **Keep an eye out** (+sharp) | Hold 3/2/1. Spend it to direct attention: an ally gets +1 choice / +1 to a roll / +1 harm / −1 harm from a danger. |
| **Do something under fire** (+cool) | Anything else risky: pick a lock, drag the wounded, hot‑wire the rig. |
| **Weird discipline moves** (+weird, costs strain) | See [04](04-moves-engine.md). |
| **Go aggro** (+hard) | Mid‑fight demand. "*Drop it or the boss loses his face.*" Enemies may surrender or flee. |

2. **Director phase.** Each enemy group acts through its **threat moves**:
   - **Warlords**: outflank, attack suddenly or cautiously, seize leverage, a show of force.
   - **Brutes**: an uncoordinated burst, a coordinated attack, defying authority.
   - **Grotesques**: an attack from stealth, insults, seizing and holding someone.
   - **Terrain**: collapse, isolating someone.

   When the PCs' moves called for an exchange of harm, the harm has already been applied. The Director phase covers what the enemy *initiates*.
3. **Morale.** NPCs whose harm is greater than or equal to their **nerve** (1–3) flee or surrender. Gangs check cohesion at harm thresholds.

**Ending a skirmish**: through objectives, a rout, a surrender (*go aggro*), or an escape (*escape a hunter*). There's **no XP for kills**. Experience comes from highlighted rolls.

## Mode 2: Road war (lanes)
**Board**: **5 lanes × 4 range bands** (*contact / close / far / gone*), on terrain that scrolls past. Obstacles come from Terrain threats: wrecks, dune ridges, broken ground, glass. Wind and dust lower visibility (−1 to *lay down fire* at *far*).

**Each rig has three stations:**
1. **Driver** makes one road move:

| Road move | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Outdistance or overtake** (+cool ± relative speed) | 10+: escape, or draw alongside. 7–9: as AW (strain: your rig takes 1‑harm ap; or go to ground or force them somewhere; or they overtake but strain). Miss: your counterpart picks against you. |
| **Deal with bad terrain** (+cool + handling) | 10+: fly through. 7–9: slow down / push too hard (v‑harm) / ditch out. |
| **Shoulder another vehicle** (+cool) | Inflict v‑harm. 10+: v‑harm +1. Miss: they shoulder you. |
| **Ram / T‑bone** (*use a vehicle as a weapon*) | A direct hit does 3‑harm + massive − target's armor; you take a glancing hit. |
| **Brake abruptly / swerve** (a Director move, but drivers can do it too) | Changes range band. Can bait a ram. |

2. **Gunner** (if crewed): *lay down fire*, or a *far* or *close* attack. Uses ammo.
3. **Boarders**: **Board a moving vehicle** (+cool − target's speed). 10+: on, and +1 forward. 7–9: on, but it costs you. A miss means you're hanging on, or down and on your own. Success **opens a deck skirmish**: a small 6×3 grid on the target rig, played to the end of that round. **Single combat** with the enemy leader can end the road war, just like UWNH's duel.

**V‑harm** = the attacker's massive − the defender's handling. The defender rolls + v‑harm. 10+: lose control (crash / spin out / two of the 7–9 list). 7–9: give ground / driven off course / take 1‑harm ap in the transmission.

**Ending a road war:**
- **Victory**: take cargo (take‑away‑their‑stuff, *your* way), take a rig (needs a driver), or take prisoners. Never slaves: freeing captives is a gig.
- **Escape**: lose the pursuers into the *gone* band.
- **Defeat**: the Director takes your stuff. You're stranded, not dead (fan rule).

## Mode 3: Gang war (abstract, with one embedded skirmish)
This is used for **sieges, raids on holds, warlord conquest and defending your hold**. It's resolved on a **siege board**: gangs as stencil blocks on a schematic of the hold. Its walls give armor (AW: a makeshift compound gives +1 armor, stone and iron give +2).

- Each **gang** has: size (*small* 10–20, *medium* about 40, *large* about 60, *huge* 100+), harm, armor, tags (savage, unruly, well‑disciplined…) and a **leader**.
- **Size steps**: +1 harm per step larger than the target, −1 per step smaller.
- **Phases** (3 to 5):
  1. **Assault a secure position**, **seize by force** or **defend something you hold**, rolled by the PC who leads (Hardholder *Leadership*: spend 1 more than your roll gives) or by the Director for NPC leaders.
  2. **The breach**: once per gang war, the PC can **drop into a skirmish** at the decisive point (the gate, the pumphouse, the leader's tent). Winning it gives the gang's next roll +1 choice.
  3. **Cohesion checks** after each exchange of harm.
- **Outcomes** feed the world turn: the hold changes hands (Lean 60 to the victor), gangs lose size, and leaders die or flee.
- **Watching or skipping**: a warlord‑against‑warlord war that you're not part of resolves offscreen in the world turn and appears in the Month Report.

## Prisoners, mercy and consequences
- Defeated named NPCs can be **released** (Rep up, and sometimes they owe you), **ransomed** (barter), **recruited** (needs Hx 0 or higher, or Inindo's "*I surrender!*"), or **killed** (fear goes up in nearby holds; their connected threats escalate).
- **Tum Tum's market** shows up in the prisoners menu as "*sell*". Choosing it is *not allowed*: the game shows Tum Tum's offer, and the only option is to refuse it, or to refuse it *with violence*. This is a deliberate design statement.
