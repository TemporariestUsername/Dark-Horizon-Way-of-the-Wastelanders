# 01 · Translation Matrix

Every system found in the teardowns ([UWNH](../research/uncharted-waters-new-horizons.md), [Inindo](../research/inindo-way-of-the-ninja.md)) and every AW element in [the source notes](../research/apocalypse-world-source-notes.md) is mapped here: kept, translated or deliberately cut.

**The rule of the split**: the game's *mechanics* come from UWNH and Inindo (tables A and B). Apocalypse World supplies the *classes, setting and ambiance* (table C), and its rules system is deliberately dropped (table D).

| Inindo system | Dark Horizon | Doc |
|---|---|---|

| 30 provinces with castles | 130 holds as the political units, each with a hardholder and a **Lean**. Crossings are the prized ones | [02](02-world.md) |
| 15 daimyo houses, about 90 generals with ratings | **6 faction hardholders plus the Choir**, sub‑faction leaders, and independent hardholders. Each lieutenant has **Brawn** and **Cunning** ratings (0–100), like Inindo's officer ratings | [10](10-warlords-and-politics.md) |
| Daimyo personality pairs, including Pro‑Nobu / Anti‑Nobu | Warlord **temperament**: Stubborn/Flexible, Steady/Reckless, Grim/Wild, **Hum‑touched/Deaf**, Warlike/Peaceful, and a **grip** (Weak/Holding/Strong/Iron) | [10](10-warlords-and-politics.md) |

## D. Apocalypse World rules → dropped
The client's direction: *the game's internal mechanics should be closer to UWNH and Inindo than to Apocalypse World*. These AW rules are **not used**:

| AW rule | Replaced by | Doc |
|---|---|---|
| 2d6 + stat moves, 10+ / 7–9 / miss | **Checks** with a shown percentage chance; battle formulas | [04](04-characters.md#checks), [09](09-combat.md) |
| Basic and battle moves as the action set | Dialogue choices, market actions, skirmish and road‑war actions | [04](04-characters.md), [09](09-combat.md) |
| Hx | Officer **loyalty** (0–100) and **affection** | [11](11-crew-and-bonds.md) |
| Highlights, experience marks, improvements | **XP on three tracks**, levels, attribute growth, abilities by level | [04](04-characters.md), [12](12-progression.md) |
| Harm clock (and 1‑harm, 2‑harm…) | **Health (HP)**, injuries, Attack and Defense | [09](09-combat.md) |
| Armor 0–2 as harm reduction | Defense and rig Armor as numbers | [09](09-combat.md) |
| Ψ‑harm, Weird strain | **Hum exposure** (0–100) and **Grit** | [04](04-characters.md#hum-exposure) |
| The MC as an AI Director, MC moves | The **event system** and the Wanderer AI | [05](05-events.md) |
| Countdown clocks, the threat map's countdowns | Faction **ambitions** (staged plans), Turning progress, the Choir's **Hum levels and reach** | [17](17-factions.md), [18](18-sub-factions.md) |
| A world clock running to 12:00 | **No clock.** The Choir spreads slowly and the campaign has no forced end | [02](02-world.md) |
| Session end (Hx reset) | The Month Report | [05](05-events.md#the-month-report) |
| Augury, insight, open your brain (as moves) | Discipline map actions and the In as a place | [04](04-characters.md#disciplines-inindos-magic-schools) |
| Sex moves | Off‑screen bonds with numeric effects | [11](11-crew-and-bonds.md#bonds-and-intimacy-offscreen) |
