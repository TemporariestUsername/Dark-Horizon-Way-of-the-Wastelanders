# Source Notes: Apocalypse World 2nd Edition (licensed)

> These are the AW elements Dark Horizon uses, taken from the *AW 2nd Ed Kickstarter Preview* playbooks file (©2k+16 D. Vincent Baker). The client holds a license, so AW's names, playbooks, moves and terms are used as they appear. This file lists them so the design docs can refer to one place.

## Aesthetic
- **Two‑tone silhouette art.** Stark white figures cut out of solid black panels, with ragged edges, as if torn or stencilled.
- **Distressed stencil display type** for headings ("THE DRIVER", "BASIC MOVES"). Body text is a clean serif, and move names are bold italic.
- **The sheet is the interface.** Name, Look, Stats, Moves, Hx, Special, Improvement, Harm countdown, Gear & Barter.
- **Voice.** Second person, profane and tender at once, wry. *"Fucked if I know."*

## Mechanics in use
- **Stats**: Cool, Hard, Hot, Sharp, Weird, each from −3 to +3. Each playbook offers its own stat arrays.
- **Basic moves**:
  - do something under fire
  - go aggro
  - sucker someone
  - do battle
  - seduce or manipulate
  - help or interfere
  - read a sitch
  - read a person
  - open your brain
  - session end
- **Peripheral moves**: harm and healing, insight, barter moves (give 1-barter with strings attached; go into a holding's bustling market; make known that you want a thing and drop jingle), and augury.
- **Battle moves**:
  - single combat
  - chaotic free‑for‑all
  - assault a secure position
  - seize by force
  - defend something you hold
  - lay down fire
  - stand overwatch
  - keep an eye out
  - bait a trap
  - hunt prey
  - escape a hunter
  - turn the tables
- **Road war moves**: outdistance or overtake, deal with bad terrain, board a moving vehicle, shoulder another vehicle, use a vehicle as a weapon, and v‑harm.
- **Harm**:
  - PCs have a 6‑segment countdown (3:00, 6:00, 9:00, 10:00, 11:00, 12:00). Harm heals on its own up to 6:00 and gets worse after 9:00 unless stabilized.
  - Untenable life: come back with −1hard, come back with +1weird, change playbook, or die.
  - NPCs, gangs and vehicles each have a 1–5 harm table.
  - Gang size steps change harm by ±1 per step. Gang cohesion depends on its leader.
- **Hx** normally sits from −2 to +3. At session end, reaching +4 resets it to +1, and dropping to −3 resets it to 0. Either reset marks experience.
- **Improvement**: 5 experience gives an improvement. After 5 improvements, advanced improvements open up.
- **MC**:
  - **Agenda**: make AW seem real, make the PCs' lives not boring, play to find out.
  - **Principles**: barf forth apocalyptica, name everyone, be a fan of the players' characters, think offscreen too, and the rest.
  - **Moves**: separate them, capture someone, put someone in a spot, trade harm for harm, announce off-screen badness, announce future badness, inflict harm, take away their stuff, make them buy, activate their stuff's downside, tell them the possible consequences and ask, offer an opportunity, turn their move back on them, make a threat move, then "*what do you do?*"
- **Threats**: Warlord, Grotesque, Brute, Affliction, Landscape, Terrain, Vehicle. Each comes with impulses and threat moves, plus countdowns, stakes questions and the threat map (rings and octants).
