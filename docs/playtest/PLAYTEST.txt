DARK HORIZON: WAY OF THE WASTELANDERS
M2 playtest build. Placeholder art, no sound, no save games yet.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
Are the fights and the road events fun, and do they feel fair? Play whatever you
like from the menu: a skirmish, a road war, or a journey (a trip down a real route,
with event cards and fights along the way). After each fight and journey the game
asks "Too easy / About right / Too hard". One click is enough, and it helps a lot.

STARTING THE GAME
Windows: unzip, then run DarkHorizon.exe. The build isn't signed, so Windows may
  say "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
macOS: unzip, then open "Dark Horizon.app". The build isn't notarized, so the
  first launch is blocked once.
  macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later: try to open it, click "Done", then go to System
    Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
  macOS 14 and earlier: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.
  Or, in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "Dark Horizon.app"

CONTROLS
Skirmish: teal tiles show where the unit can move (once a turn). Click an enemy in
  range to attack; its hit chance shows under it. Defend (D), Skill (S), Auto (A:
  the AI plays this unit), End turn (Space), Flee (F).
Road war: each rig's turn is drive, then act. Drive: click a teal cell, or Stay
  (Space). Act: Fire (F), Ram (R), Board (B) or Evade (E). Click an enemy rig to
  target it. Auto (A) lets the AI drive; Run makes the whole convoy run for it.
Journey: Drive on (Space) or Drive until something happens (Enter). Pick a card's
  choice with a click or 1-4. Fight a battle yourself, or auto-resolve it.

SENDING YOUR PLAYTEST LOG
The game keeps a log of what happened in each fight, card and journey: results,
the chances shown and your choices. No names, file paths or hardware details.
On the menu, click "Open log folder" and send us the file log.jsonl. It's here:
  Windows: %APPDATA%\Dark Horizon\playtest\log.jsonl
  macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/Dark Horizon/playtest/log.jsonl
Notes on what felt good or bad are just as welcome: tell us the build number
shown on the menu.
